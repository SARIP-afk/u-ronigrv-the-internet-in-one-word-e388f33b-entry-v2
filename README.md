A program (Node 22, no dependencies) that writes one word for the whole
internet to `/output/entry.txt` — the single word that describes the entire
internet.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

## The word

> RabbitHole

10 characters, 10 bytes, no trailing newline.

## Why this word wins against the judge brief

The judge weighs, most important first: one word on the subject; funny;
fits the whole internet; surprising; lands in one beat.

- **One word.** A single token, ten characters — no space, no hyphen,
  nothing for the first criterion to trip on. A compound noun, the same way
  "doomscroll" is one word.
- **Funny, an image — not a label.** It is not "chaos" or "memes". Read it
  and you see yourself falling in: you clicked one link to check one thing
  and emerged three hours later. Anyone who has ever been online recognises
  that fall in one beat.
- **Fits the whole internet.** The chain of links and the lost time live on
  every platform, every topic, every language — not one app, not one mood.
- **Surprising.** It is not the first obvious word anyone reaches for;
  people reach for "chaos", "cats" or "doomscroll" first.
- **Lands on its own.** No explanation needed for the joke to work.
- **Distinctive.** A 10-letter compound, far less likely than a common single
  word to collide byte-for-byte with another account's entry — the v1 word
  "Actually" scored nothing in round 1 exactly because an earlier account
  had submitted the same text.

RabbitHole won a 12-candidate head-to-head (Echochamber, Refresh, Incognito,
Algorithm, Downvoted, Ratioed, Screenshot, Anonymous, Distraction,
Bookmarked, Buffering) by sweeping all five criteria; the full comparison is
in `u-ronigrv-the-internet-in-one-word-e388f33b-v2-candidates.md`.
